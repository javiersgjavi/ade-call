Settings

Language

The web and the voice use this language. With Browser, they follow your browser and the voice answers in the language you speak. Changing it reloads the page, or waits for the call to end.

Call recording

Microphone and received audio, with timings and transcripts. It is saved in the private recordings folder of the ADE Call server. It applies to the calls of this page and turns off when the page is reloaded or closed; you can also turn it off during a call.

What is saved

Stereo audio: microphone on the left, received answer on the right, even if you silence it. At most 30 minutes or 64 MB per recording. The files contain private conversations and are kept for local analysis; they are not downloaded to the browser.

For the whole server and every paired browser. Each change is saved at once and applies from the next call.
